LevelsofEnterpriseAI

AI for daily, open-ended knowledge work.

Vivek Haldar levelsofai.fyi

The scope

Daily, open-ended
knowledge work.

The work that fills the space between the documented processes.

Separate from high-frequency SOP tasks and software engineering that ships production code.

Carrot

Smoother, less stressful work.

More time for creative and strategic work.

Stick

Agent fluency becomes a basic prerequisite.

Like fluency with Word, Excel and PowerPoint.

The scale

The level numbers are linear.

The effort, impact and benefits are

exponential

Much like a job ladder.
